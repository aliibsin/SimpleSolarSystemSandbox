Simple Solar System Sandbox

This is a simple sandbox of our solar system featuring the sun and the main 8 planets. Click and drag the mouse to rotate. Use the mouse wheel to zoom. Click a planet or use the Focus Camera controls to follow it and learn more. Control the planet scale, time scale, and orbit visibility through the controls at the top right.

Scales (km, s)

Solar System Size: 1/10,000
Sun: 1/1,000
Planets: 1/1,000 minimum
Time: 1/3600 minimum
